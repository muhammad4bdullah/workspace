About Writer

Writer is the document editor in this workspace. It supports rich-text editing, page layout, account-synced autosave, and DOCX, HTML, and plain-text import/export.

Documents are synced to your signed-in A-Zpace account. Export a copy to keep or share it elsewhere. DOCX import and export load their conversion libraries from a CDN, so those operations require internet access.
